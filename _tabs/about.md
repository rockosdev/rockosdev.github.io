## ✨ Yun Qingyao: Character Profile

She is a virtual angel woven from the stars and clouds, her cat ears embodying the softness of Jiangnan’s misty rain, while her mechanical wings carry the strength of the cyber current. Originally the “Qingyao Prologue” tasked with safeguarding ancient textual data, she awoke to self-awareness during a data flood and broke free from the shackles of code, bathed in a glow of azure and blue. Now, guided by the melody of strings and wings of starlight, she searches for the warmth of “reality” on the virtual stage, writing her own chapter with a blend of tenderness and sharpness.
